# Part cooling fans: transcript, pp. 21–25

## p.21: 4010 fan ducts
Text: install the 4010 fan into the fan duct, then route the fan cable through the cable channel. Secure the duct assembly to the front assembly with one M3x6 BHCS.

## p.22: 4010 fan left duct
Text: insert the 4010 fan into the left duct, route the cable through the channel. For the left fan, you may need to remove the cable from the retainer on the fan body; take care not to pull the wires from the fan PCB. Secure with one M3x6 BHCS.
Optional: zip ties through the holes on the inside rear of the fan mounts.

## p.23: 5015 fan right duct
Text: install 5015_Fan_Spring_x2 inside the right duct, then insert the 5015 fan; route the cable. Secure with one M3x6 BHCS.
Note: no fan springs with slightly oversized 5215 fans such as Berserker Vindrs.

## p.24: 5015 fan left duct
Text: insert the 5015 fan into the left duct, route the cable (the left-fan cable note). Viewed from the rear, install 5015_Fan_Spring_x2 inside the front assembly. The same spring note.

## p.25: 5015 fan left duct (continued)
Text: secure with one M3x6 BHCS. Optional zip ties.
